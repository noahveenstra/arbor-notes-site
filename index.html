      Data & privacy

      How Arbor handles your data

      Arbor reads and writes notes, proofreading dictionary entries, and keyboard shortcut preferences in the Apple account you sign in with. Shortcut preferences contain Arbor command names and key combinations, not note content. Signing out of this browser removes notes, metadata, dictionary entries, shortcut preferences, and search data cached here. The app shell and Arbor’s public search model stay on this device. They do not include your notes. Notes and dictionary entries stored in iCloud stay in iCloud.

      The name in Settings stays in this browser. It is not written to iCloud. Each word of that name is added to the proofreading dictionary, and those dictionary entries sync with the Apple Account you sign in with. A note’s revision history on this browser keeps the name you were using when that revision was saved.

      Recently opened notes are cached unencrypted in this browser. Signing out deletes that cache. It does not delete your iCloud notes. A few edits that have not reached iCloud yet are also kept unencrypted here until they save or you sign out. Clearing site data or running low on storage can erase an edit before it reaches iCloud. Safari can also clear a site's data after seven days of Safari use without interaction with the site.

      Ignoring a proofreading suggestion stores the marked text and a short excerpt on either side in this browser. Ignored words and counts of accepted or dismissed suggestions also stay on this device, in the IndexedDB database arbor-web-proofreading-learning. Signing out deletes the database.

      To connect to iCloud, this page loads Apple’s CloudKit JS library from cdn.apple-cloudkit.com. It runs in this page alongside the note editor. Apple serves and controls that library; Arbor does not host or pin its contents. Sign-in and note requests go directly from this browser to Apple’s CloudKit service.
