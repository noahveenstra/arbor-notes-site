Data & privacy

Arbor reads and writes notes and proofreading dictionary entries in the Apple account you sign in with. Signing out of this browser removes notes, metadata, dictionary entries, and search data cached here. The app shell and Arbor’s public search model stay on this device. They do not include your notes. Notes and dictionary entries stored in iCloud stay in iCloud.

To connect to iCloud, this page loads Apple’s CloudKit JS library from cdn.apple-cloudkit.com. It runs in this page alongside the note editor. Apple serves and controls that library; Arbor does not host or pin its contents. Sign-in and note requests go directly from this browser to Apple’s CloudKit service.

    Connect Arbor to iCloud

    Add your CloudKit API token to app/cloudkit-config.js to connect this page to the signed-in iCloud account’s private Arbor database.

    1

    Open app/cloudkit-config.js in the arbor-site repository.

    2

    Replace the placeholder token and choose the environment your Arbor app syncs to.

    3

    Save the file and reload this page, then choose Sign in with Apple.
